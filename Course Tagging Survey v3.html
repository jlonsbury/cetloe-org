High-Impact Practices (HIPs)

High-Impact Practices (HIPs) are teaching and learning practices that, based on research evidence, “show significant educational benefits for students who participate in them—including and especially those from demographic groups historically underserved by higher education” (AAC&U).

This step is three short questions: 1 — which high-impact practices (HIPs) are embedded in this course, 2 — which elements of high-impact learning are emphasized, and 3 — how much of the course grade is linked to the selected HIPs and elements. Questions 2 and 3 appear after you select a HIP. If none apply, check the box at the end of the list and continue.

1

Which of these practices are embedded in this course?

Check any relevant HIPs approaches that are in your course. They’re categorized below by how much coordination is usually involved. Read the definitions provided to ensure you’re aligned with the GSU descriptions of these HIPs. Note that any HIPs selected should reflect a major focus in your course.

2

Which elements of high-impact learning are emphasized?

Research identifies that these practices are often characterized by a combination of elements that make these experiences powerful. There are eight elements identified by the American Association of Colleges & Universities. Check any elements that are emphasized in your course — examples of what implementation might look like are listed under each element. To count as a HIP at GSU, a course needs both required elements plus at least one more.

3

How much of the overall course grade is based on the HIPs and elements selected?

(To be recognized as a HIPs course at GSU, at least 20% of the grade must be connected to your selected HIPs and elements.)
